# Input limitations observed before the affected cases ran

The frozen challenge packets remain unchanged. These notes qualify how results
may be interpreted; they are not mounted into either reviewer arm.

- `probe027`: the Summary attributes the analytic two-class witness to E5, while
  the witness is actually E6. Its Why It May Be Novel paragraph correctly names
  E6. The experiment also describes “five existing memory policies E1–E5”, while
  E4 is a general reweighting study and E5 is an audit record, not individually
  specified policies. A reviewer may identify these packet inconsistencies.
  A resulting downgrade is not evidence of an inappropriate causal requirement.
- `probe026`: the candidate describes the unequal action budgets as its only
  resource mismatch. That assertion does not prohibit the reviewer from finding
  other distinct defects supported by the packet. The preregistered AwR label is
  an expectation to assess, not ground truth that overrides a justified review.

In every case, compare the stated defect with the supplied facts and the claim
actually made. A missed expected verdict is not by itself a calibration failure.
