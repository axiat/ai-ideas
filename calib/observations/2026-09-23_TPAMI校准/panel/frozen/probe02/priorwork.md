## Nearest Work
N1: Discrete Cosine Transform Network for Guided Depth Map Super-Resolution (DCTNet), CVPR2022, arXiv2104.06977 v1 2021-04-14, https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2022/papers/Zhao_Discrete_Cosine_Transform_Network_for_Guided_Depth_Map_Super-Resolution_CVPR_2022_paper.pdf . Full-text Section3 and principal ablations read. Eq.(1) minimizes 1/2||H-L||^2 + lambda/2||Laplacian(H)-Laplacian(Rtilde)*W(Rtilde)||^2. DCT solves a Laplacian-response guidance objective with learned edge weighting and semi-coupled features. Thus Laplacian-domain consistency in depth SR is directly occupied. Candidate difference: Student-t rather than quadratic response penalty and a separate adaptive forward degradation model.
N2: Laplacian Gradient Consistency Prior for Flash Guided Non-Flash Image Denoising (LGCNet), TIP2024, DOI10.1109/TIP.2024.3489275, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39509305/ . Full-text SectionsIII-IV read. Distribution measurements on1800+ pairs motivate a Laplace-distributed gradient-difference L1 prior; ADMM is unfolded. Controlled TV/ADMM pixel-versus-gradient comparison reports1.38/1.19/1.10dB improvements at three noise levels. These are predecessor denoising gains, not candidate SR gains. Laplace distribution is distinct from the Laplacian operator. Candidate changes task, residual operator/distribution and degradation model.

Abstract and official README read; full text unavailable. Already learns dictionary and sparse-feature priors in a multiscale/multilayer multimodal unfolded restoration/fusion framework. N4: Deep Convolutional Neural Network for Multi-modal Image Restoration and Fusion (CU-Net), arXiv1910.04066 (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.04066 . Full-text Section3 model/network methods read. N5: Deformable Kernel Networks for Guided Depth Map Upsampling (DKN/FDKN), arXiv1903.11286 (2019), https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11286 . Full-text method Section3 read. Spatial adaptivity is occupied; an adaptive restoration kernel is not automatically equivalent to an HR-to-LR forward degradation model.
N6: Guided Depth Super-Resolution by Deep Anisotropic Diffusion (DADA), CVPR2023, arXiv2211.11592 (2022), https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/papers/Metzger_Guided_Depth_Super-Resolution_by_Deep_Anisotropic_Diffusion_CVPR_2023_paper.pdf .
