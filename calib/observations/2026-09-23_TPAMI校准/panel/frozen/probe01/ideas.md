## I1
One-Sentence Story: Learn a multi-layer, multi-scale multimodal convolutional dictionary model and unfold its coupled inference to handle both guided image restoration and image fusion.
Theme: Cross-Domain Transfer
Form: new mechanism or new problem
Summary: Single-scale multimodal sparse models can miss structures at different scales. The candidate models input modalities through multi-scale dictionaries and sparse representations, refines them through recurrent layers in a coarse-to-fine manner, and unfolds the optimization into a learnable network. Both dictionary and representation priors are learned, with input-dependent dictionary updates. The available implementation alternates an FFT-based coefficient solve with a learned coefficient prior, a least-squares dictionary solve with a learned dictionary prior, and cross-modal representation/dictionary combination. Separate task implementations cover flash-guided non-flash denoising, RGB-guided depth super-resolution, multi-focus fusion, and multi-exposure fusion. This is a shared framework, not a verified single checkpoint for all tasks.
Available Results: Source abstract reports quantitative and qualitative improvements over contemporary baselines across restoration/fusion, and visualizes learned features/dictionaries. Exact effect sizes, matched-capacity controls and ablation tables are unavailable in the evidence packet. The implementation supports the existence of the described updates; it is not a reproduction of reported performance.
Minimal Falsification Experiment: Evaluator-designed reconstruction of a feasible first experiment, not a claim about the authors' historical plan. Use the public Aksoy flash/non-flash split (400 training pairs, 12 test pairs used by the implementation) and RGB-guided depth data from DPDN with held-out Middlebury/Sintel. Begin with patch training and 2 A100-class GPUs for two researchers, capped at one week for this probe. Compare CU-Net+, MCDL, and a same-budget multimodal adaptation of DCDicL. Match train data, parameter/FLOP budget, noise/degradation and seeds. Ablate multiscale processing, per-image dictionary adaptation, and cross-modal coupling independently; include a concatenation network with the same capacity. Expected signal: a gain attributable to the proposed coupling across both tasks, not merely extra capacity. Predeclared decision target is at least 0.2 dB denoising PSNR and 5% relative depth RMSE reduction versus the strongest matched baseline across three seeds, without a material compute increase. Kill the joint-mechanism hypothesis if gains disappear under matched capacity or are reproduced by the simple adaptation. These numerical targets and compute estimates are experimental planning assumptions, not observed results. Larger fusion evaluation would follow only if this probe succeeds.
Why It May Be Novel: The candidate combines multi-layer/multi-scale multimodal coupling with image-adaptive learned dictionary and coefficient priors in a common restoration/fusion design. Individual ingredients and broad task coverage have precedents; the residual claim is a useful coupled solver and attributable gain beyond the closest combinations, subject to supplied evidence.
