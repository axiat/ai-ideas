## I1
One-Sentence Story: Learn RGB-depth-saliency phase consistency and unfold phase updates before reconstructing a salient-object segmentation map.
Theme: Cross-Domain Transfer
Form: new mechanism or new problem
Summary: The candidate hypothesizes that discrepancies between phase-related representations of RGB/depth inputs and the desired saliency output approximately follow a Laplace distribution. It builds a three-way consistency prior, updates a latent output-phase representation using sparse/learned regularization, then maps updated phase and the input modalities to a saliency mask. In the inspected implementation, input FFT phases are converted to a phase-only reconstruction by IFFT with unit magnitude; the update includes learned weighted combination, soft thresholding, auxiliary/dual-variable-like updates and a learned regularizer. The phase-to-image module fuses RGB, depth and updated phase features through three Swin branches and attention. It is not merely a raw inverse Fourier transform. The exact statistical variable and mathematical correspondence between code and the unavailable manuscript remain unverified.
Available Results: Source abstract reports quantitative and qualitative superiority on nine RGB-D salient-object datasets. Numeric effect sizes, model-size controls, detailed residual fit statistics and intervention/ablation tables are unavailable to this panel. Official code was read, not executed.
Minimal Falsification Experiment: Evaluator-designed bounded test, not a statement about the authors' historical plan. Use fixed documented NJU2K/NLPR training splits and held-out STERE/LFSD tests with saliency masks; adopt the same backbone, pretraining, training data and schedule for every arm. A two-researcher team and 2 A100-class GPUs run a one-week initial probe with three seeds and patch/resolution controls. Compare a same-capacity three-branch fusion baseline, phase-only reconstruction with no learned consistency updates, learned phase updates without the Laplace penalty, and Gaussian/Laplace residual versions. Include the strongest reproducible contemporary RGB-D baseline and a fairly adapted DFENet frequency baseline. Evaluate S-measure, weighted F-measure and MAE under clean, corrupted-depth and modest misregistration conditions. Planning target: at least0.01 absolute S-measure and5% relative MAE improvement over the strongest same-budget control on two held-out sets without worse corruption sensitivity. Kill the phase-consistency hypothesis if matched-capacity controls reproduce gains or phase interventions do not change the claimed mechanism signal; better histogram fit alone is insufficient. Budget/thresholds are reconstructed assumptions, not observed source results. Nine-dataset evaluation follows only after this attribution probe succeeds.
Why It May Be Novel: Phase-based saliency, frequency fusion and Laplace cross-modal priors each have precedents. Potential novelty is a task-output-related RGB-depth-saliency consistency model whose learned phase update contributes independently beyond those components. Its existence and gain must be assessed separately under the supplied evidence.
