## Candidate evidence

No numeric gain or attributed ablation appears in the recovered abstract.

## Nearest Work
N1: Laplacian Gradient Consistency Prior for Flash Guided Non-Flash Image Denoising (LGCNet), TIP2024, DOI10.1109/TIP.2024.3489275, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39509305/ . Full-text SectionsIII-IV and official update code read across the research team. Models cross-modal gradient residuals with a Laplace/L1 prior and unfolds ADMM with shrinkage/auxiliary/dual updates. The statistical-prior-to-unrolled-network route is established. Candidate difference: phase-related rather than gradient representation, a semantic saliency-mask output domain and constraints relating both input-phase representations to the output. N1 already includes observation x, guidance y and latent output z; an input/output relation itself is not newly introduced. This is not proof the resulting objectives are identical.
N2: DFENet: Deep Fourier-embedded Network for RGB and Thermal Salient Object Detection, arXiv2411.18409v2 dated2025-02-10, https://arxiv.org/html/2411.18409v2 . Abstract and SectionsIII-A-E read. Processes Fourier amplitude and phase, enhances phase/contour features, and weights prediction-versus-label frequency errors using multimodal phase information. Thus learned phase, cross-modal frequency fusion and input/output spectral association have precedents. Available v2 focuses on four RGB-T datasets and does not establish the candidate's RGB-depth-saliency Laplace update model. Exclude v3 dated2025-11-04 (later renamed FreqSal and expanded tasks).
N3: Frequency-aware feature aggregation network with dual-task consistency for RGB-T salient object detection (FFANet), Pattern Recognition146,2024, DOI10.1016/j.patcog.2023.110043, https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0031320323007409 . Publisher abstract/highlights read, method-unread. Aggregates high/low-frequency complementary cues and uses high-frequency signed-distance-map prediction with region/contour task consistency. Establishes frequency plus consistency as a known direction, not identity with the candidate's full phase objective.
N4: WaveNet: Wavelet Network With Knowledge Distillation for RGB-T Salient Object Detection, TIP2023, https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10127616/ ; official implementation https://github.com/nowander/WaveNet/blob/af3c9423/networks/Wavenet.py . N5: Visual Saliency Detection of RGB-D Data Based on Spatial-Frequency Mixed-Domain Analysis, Yue etal, Robot39(5),2017, DOI10.13973/j.cnki.robot.2017.0652, https://robot.sia.cn/cn/article/pdf/preview/10.13973/j.cnki.robot.2017.0652.pdf . RGB-D frequency fusion and phase-preserving saliency reconstruction are directly prior. Candidate adds learned output-phase consistency and a supervised neural mapping.
N6: Spatio-temporal Saliency Detection Using Phase Spectrum of Quaternion Fourier Transform (PFT/PQFT), CVPR2008, https://iacl.ece.jhu.edu/proceedings/cvpr2008/papers/375.pdf . Global spectral reconstruction is established. It does not by itself occupy the candidate's learned output-phase constraint.
